FAQ

Is this a donation?

Yes. It's a voluntary contribution to support Mr Wheelz. Because it goes to the creator rather than a qualified charity, it is not a tax deductible charitable donation.

Where does my donation go?

Mostly behind-the-scenes costs: captions, editing, getting to stories, and keeping the gear working.

Can I cancel monthly support?

Yes. Use the Manage monthly support button on the device where you subscribed, or email info@rollflow.net for help.

How will I pay?

Enter your card securely in the Stripe payment form on this page.

What if there's a billing problem?

Contact info@rollflow.net for help.
